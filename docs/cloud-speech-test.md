# Cloud speech and note quality check

1. Create a Groq Free Plan key and open **AI connections** from the gear icon.
2. Paste the Groq key. Keep the NVIDIA key already saved, and select Nemotron 3.5 Super for note quality.
3. Choose one short, non-sensitive lesson whose teacher consented to external processing.
4. Turn on **Cloud transcription**, tick the one-job audio consent, then choose **Transcribe**.
5. Compare technical names, important numbers and the final minute against the recording. Record elapsed time and peak CPU/RAM in Task Manager.
6. Tick the separate NVIDIA transcript consent and generate the PDF.
7. Confirm every captured slide appears with explanation from the same time in the lesson. An unmatched image must say so instead of showing invented text.
8. Confirm greetings, attendance, repeated filler, timestamps and slide numbers are absent while subject-relevant values, units and formulas remain.
9. Revoke or rotate the test keys after evaluation if desired. A 429 means wait for the Free Plan reset; completed speech and note parts remain cached.
